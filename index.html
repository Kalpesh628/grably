faq

Straight answers.

Is Grably free?+
Yes. No signup, no watermark, and no download limits on our side. The fetch itself runs through our own private relay.
Which links work?+
YouTube first — watch pages, youtu.be links, Shorts, live replays. Many other public video pages (TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, Reddit, Vimeo) work through the same engine, marked beta because results vary.
How does the timeline trimmer work?+
Drag the two handles to mark your section, preview the loop, then download just that clip. The cut runs entirely on your device — the video is streamed, trimmed locally, and never uploaded anywhere.
Why did my video fail?+
Private, members-only, age-restricted, region-locked or DRM-protected videos can't be fetched — no downloader can reach those. Our relay can also be busy at peak times; wait a minute and retry.
Do you keep my files or links?+
No. Files pass through a transient relay and are never stored by us. Your recent links live only in your own browser's local storage — clear them any time.
Is this legal?+
Only download videos you own, have permission to save, or that are explicitly free to reuse. Respect creators and YouTube's Terms — this page isn't legal advice.
